# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026

## What we collect

When you use Pattern Thief without an account, we collect a browser fingerprint and an anonymous cookie ID to prevent abuse of the free tier. We hash your IP address (we never store it directly) for the same reason. We do not collect or store the content of your prompts.

When you create an account, we collect your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile info from Google.

When you purchase a lifetime subscription, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment details. Pattern Thief never sees or stores your full payment card information.

We store the patterns and cards you save, and the original problem statements you typed when you saved them, so we can show them back to you across devices.

## What we do with it

We use this information to:
- Operate Pattern Thief and enforce the free tier limit
- Sync your saved cards across your devices
- Process your payment and provide refunds within the 14-day window
- Send you transactional emails (welcome, receipts, refund confirmations)
- Communicate updates about Pattern Thief (you can opt out anytime)

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties beyond what is necessary to provide the service (Stripe for payments, Supabase for data storage, Resend for email, Anthropic for AI inference).

Your prompts are sent to Anthropic to generate patterns. We do not store the full prompt text on our servers beyond the original_problem you saved with a card.

## Your rights

You can:
- Delete your account at any time by emailing Prashant@BeyondSingular.com
- Request a refund within 14 days of purchase, automatically from your account
- Request a copy of your data by emailing Prashant@BeyondSingular.com
- Unsubscribe from non-transactional emails

## Cookies

We use a single anonymous cookie (`pt_anon`) to prevent abuse of the free tier. Authenticated sessions use a Supabase session cookie. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

## Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, email Prashant@BeyondSingular.com.
